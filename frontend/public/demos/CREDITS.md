# Credits for the demo recordings in the app

The files in `mix/`, `repair/` and `produce/` are the browser demos' own recordings, decoded unchanged from their JavaScript data packs by `scripts/demo-media.py` so the app's Home > Demos can open them as sessions. Where each came from, its licence and what was changed are listed in:

- `mix/`: [demo/CREDITS.md](../../../demo/CREDITS.md) (NASA launch commentary, LibriVox readings of Jules Verne, Satie by Robin Alciatore from Musopen, NASA Cassini Saturn radio emissions)
- `repair/`: [demos/repair/CREDITS.md](../../../demos/repair/CREDITS.md) (Original Dixieland Jass Band 1917 and Caruso 1907 from Wikimedia Commons, public domain; plus two files of the first demo)
- `produce/`: [demos/produce/CREDITS.md](../../../demos/produce/CREDITS.md) (CC0 stems and loops from the Internet Archive, the Satie recording and two NASA sounds)

All are public domain or CC0 as stated there. `peaks.json` in each folder is derived from the files, for the thumbnails.
